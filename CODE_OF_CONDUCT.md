# Code Of Conduct

Worktree Console should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying clear about technical concerns.
- Keep feedback focused on code, design, documentation, and project goals.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask contributors to share private local paths, logs, credentials, or machine-specific registry files unless they choose to provide redacted examples.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to run unsafe commands.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block participants; or take other reasonable moderation actions when needed.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile or request a private contact path in a minimal public issue.
